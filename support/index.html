Support

A question, a missing card, or a rate that looks wrong? Write to us. For a rate, a link to the bank’s terms helps us fix it quickly.

Email support@pointly.in

Common questions

Which cards does Pointly support?

Popular cards from American Express, Axis Bank, HDFC Bank, HSBC, ICICI Bank and SBI Card, with more on the way. New cards and rate changes reach the app by themselves, without an App Store update. If yours is missing, tell us which one.

How are the points worked out?

From each bank’s published terms. Tap ⓘ on any result to see the rule, the arithmetic and a link to the bank’s page. They’re estimates: banks exclude some merchants and change rates, and your statement has the final word.

Do I need to give my card number or bank login?

No. You pick the card type, such as “HDFC Regalia Gold”, and that’s all. Pointly never asks for a card number, CVV or login.

Where is my data kept?

On your iPhone. There’s no account, and we can’t see your cards or payments. The privacy page has the details.

How do I delete a payment?

Open it in History and tap Delete payment. What it added to the card’s limits comes off too. Deleting the app removes everything.

Is there an Android app?

Not yet; it’s coming. Pointly is for iPhone, on iOS 26 or later.
